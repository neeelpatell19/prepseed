THE PROBLEM

Every growing store runs on channels that don't talk to each other.

Inventory drifts across every channel

Stock updates lag between the website, marketplaces and the store counter, so an item sells twice — or shows in stock when it isn't.

Carts are abandoned and never chased

Most shoppers leave at checkout, and most stores have no automated way to bring them back.

Catalog changes wait on a developer

A price change or a new variant means raising a ticket instead of just editing a page.

Orders scatter across five dashboards

Website, WhatsApp, Instagram and marketplace orders never land in one place.

COD & courier reconciliation is a spreadsheet

NDRs, RTOs and COD remittance get tracked manually, order by order.

Marketing spend isn't tied to revenue

Coupons and campaigns run without ever confirming which one actually drove a sale.

Customer history resets on every channel

A repeat buyer on Instagram looks like a total stranger on the website.

Returns & refunds run over email

No unified workflow for pickup, quality check and refund status.
